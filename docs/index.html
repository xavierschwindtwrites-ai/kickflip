Everything you need to plan a campaign

Ten tools in one app. Fill them in order and your campaign plan builds itself.

Per-backer margin calculator Improved

See exactly what you net after Kickstarter fees, printing, and shipping before you launch. Digital-only tiers (ebook, audiobook, PDF) skip print and shipping costs entirely.

Three-scenario modeler Improved

Plan for conservative, expected, and breakout funding — now with custom per-tier backer distribution so your projections match your real reward mix.

Shipping planner

Know your total shipping exposure across every region — US, UK, EU, Canada, Australia, and Rest of World — before you set your goal.

Drag-and-drop stretch goals Improved

Build a stretch goal ladder that is funded by the backers who unlock it — then drag goals into the right order with a single move.

Multi-printer fulfillment New

Split fulfillment across multiple printers, assign tiers to each, and see per-entry and aggregate costs in one place.

Campaign management New

Rename, duplicate, or delete campaigns from the sidebar. Run multiple campaigns side-by-side for different books or editions.

Pre-launch follower tracker New

Enter your Kickstarter pre-launch follower count and see exactly how many you need to hit each funding scenario, using industry-standard conversion rate math.

Live campaign tracker New

Log daily backer and funding totals while your campaign runs. See a funding chart, daily average, and projected final total at a glance.

Launch readiness check

A checklist that pulls from your real data — pricing, shipping, printer quotes, and outreach — to tell you if you’re ready to go live.

Post-campaign retrospective

Compare actual costs and backer counts to your projections. Carry the lessons into your next campaign instead of starting from scratch.

Getting started

Download the app for your platform and you’re up and running in under two minutes.

Mac

  1. Download the .dmg file
  2. Open it and drag KickFlip to your Applications folder
  3. Try to open KickFlip — macOS will show a security warning
  4. Open Terminal (Command + Space, type Terminal, press Enter)
  5. Paste this command and press Enter:
    xattr -cr /Applications/KickFlip.app
  6. Double-click KickFlip in Applications — it opens normally from now on

This one-time step is required because KickFlip is not yet signed with an Apple Developer certificate. You’ll never need to run it again.

Windows

  1. Download the .exe installer
  2. Run it — click “More info” then “Run anyway” if SmartScreen appears
  3. Launch KickFlip from the Start menu
  4. Start planning your campaign

The SmartScreen prompt appears because KickFlip isn’t yet code-signed. It’s safe to proceed — the source code is fully open on GitHub.

Built for authors, by an author

KickFlip is a free community tool. I built it while planning my own Kickstarter campaign because I couldn’t find anything that answered the questions I actually had — what will I net per backer, how many followers do I need, what happens if I hit breakout? It will always be free.
